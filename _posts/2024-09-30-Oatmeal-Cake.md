This recipe can be made with either two cups leftover porridge or, pour 1¼ c boiling water over 1 c rolled oats and let stand for five minutes.

Cream:
- ½ c butter
- ½ brown sugar

  Add:
  - 2 eggs, one at a time and continue to cream.
 
Add oats/porridge and mix.
 
Add dry mixture:
- 1 c flour
- 1 t soda
- 1 t salt
- 1 t cinamon
- ½ t cloves
- 1 t cream of tarte
- 1 c chopped dates
- ½ c chopped walnuts

  Mix and pour into greased 12 X 8 pan.

  Bake at 350 degrees for 40 - 45 min.
