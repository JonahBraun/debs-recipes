Use your collection of bread heels and or stale bread for this savory compliment to stews, soups or baked beans.

- 5 slices (or the equivalent) bread of any kind. Sourdough is nice
- 3 egss
- ¼ c grated parmesan and/or cheddar cheese
- 1¼ c milk or mix of milk and cream
- ½  t pepper 
- ½ t salt
- 1 T melted butter to grease the pan 
- ½ c finely minced fresh herbs of your choice (optional

Place bread in greased 9 X 9 pan. Whisk all other ingredients together and pour over bread. Allow to sit at least 30 minutes or several hours in the fridge.

Toppings:
- ½ grated parmesan cheese
- 1 T melted butter
- ½ cherry tomatoes
- ¼ sliced chives or green onions
  
- Just before baking top with more grated cheese and melted butter.

 Bake for 35 - 40 minutes until set. Serve hot topped with halved cherry tomatoes and sliced chives or green onions.
