A family favorite from way back.

Cream:
- 2 c sugar
- ½ butter
- 2 eggs
- 2 t vanilla

Mix dry ingredients.
- ½ c cocoa
- 2½ c flour
- 2 t baking powder
- 2 t baking soda

Boil 2 c water then add water and dry ingredients alternately to creamed mixture.

Pour batter into greased 8 X 12 baking pan. Bake at 350 for about 35 minutes.
